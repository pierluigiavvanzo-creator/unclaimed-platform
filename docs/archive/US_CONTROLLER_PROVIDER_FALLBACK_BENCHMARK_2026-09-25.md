## 1. Official Wyoming baseline

Authoritative state facts currently verified from the Wyoming Secretary of State:

- domestic LLC Articles of Organization filing fee: USD 100;

- online filing may include a card/convenience surcharge;
- annual report is due on the first day of the LLC anniversary month.
