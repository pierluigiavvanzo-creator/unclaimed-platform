## 1. Official Wyoming baseline

Authoritative state facts currently verified from the Wyoming Secretary of State:

- domestic LLC Articles of Organization filing fee: USD 100;
- annual report/license tax: USD 60 minimum, or USD 0.0002 per dollar of Wyoming assets, whichever is greater;
- registered agent with a physical Wyoming address is required;
- online filing may include a card/convenience surcharge;
- annual report is due on the first day of the LLC anniversary month.

Official sources reviewed:

- https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/docs/BusinessFees.pdf
- https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Business/LLC/LLC-ArticlesOrganization.pdf

Economic implication:

Any provider quote materially above the state baseline must be justified by registered-agent service, privacy/address handling, EIN assistance, support, compliance tooling or other real operational value.
