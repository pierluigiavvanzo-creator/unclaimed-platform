## 1. Current verified public facts

Mercury currently states that:

- the company must be formed and registered in the United States or a U.S. territory;
- founders do not need to be U.S. citizens or residents;
- the business must have existing or planned U.S. operations;
- the principal/physical business address may be a verifiable U.S. or international physical address;
- a registered-agent address, PO box, virtual address or mail center is not accepted as the physical operating address;
- Mercury may require documentary proof of U.S. operations;
- account approval remains subject to Mercury/bank underwriting and KYC/KYB.

Official references reviewed on 2026-10-05:

- https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28770467511060-Eligibility-and-requirements-for-opening-a-Mercury-account
- https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28769699533588-Company-address-requirements
- https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/53171397356820-Verifying-your-U-S-business-operations

This pack records current public requirements only. It does not guarantee approval.

## 5. U.S. operations evidence plan

Mercury states that some businesses without physical U.S. ties may need proof of legitimate U.S. operations.

Examples Mercury currently lists include:

- sale to a U.S.-based customer;
- invoice or contract with a U.S.-based customer, partner or supplier;
- signed investment agreement;
- U.S.-based employee documentation;
- U.S. physical office/coworking lease;
- recent utility bill;
- recent U.S. federal tax return.

`CURRENT_VERIFIED_US_OPERATIONS_EVIDENCE = NOT_YET_BOUND`
