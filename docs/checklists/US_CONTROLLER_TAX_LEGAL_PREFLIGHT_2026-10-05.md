## 1. Current authoritative public facts

### EIN / SSN / ITIN

Current IRS Form SS-4 instructions state that when the responsible party does not have and is ineligible to obtain an SSN or ITIN, line 7b may use `foreign` or `N/A`.

The IRS Internal Revenue Manual also states that foreign individuals are not required to have an ITIN in order to receive an EIN under that fact pattern.

References reviewed 2026-10-05:

- https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4
- https://www.irs.gov/irm/part21/irm_21-007-013r

Therefore a provider statement that an ITIN is universally required before an EIN is not consistent with current IRS guidance.

### Foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity reporting

Current IRS Form 5472 instructions state that a foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity can be required to file Form 5472 attached to a pro-forma Form 1120.

Applicability, reportable transactions, deadlines and the owner's wider tax position remain fact-specific and require professional review.

Reference:

- https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472

### FinCEN BOI

FinCEN currently states that U.S. companies are exempt from BOI reporting under the final rule updated in August 2026.

Reference reviewed 2026-10-05:

- https://www.fincen.gov/boi

This must be rechecked at formation because regulatory rules can change.

### Wyoming annual report

Wyoming currently permits active entities to file annual reports online.

The license tax for ordinary LLCs is USD 60 minimum or USD 0.0002 of assets located and employed in Wyoming, whichever is greater.

References:

- https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/OnlineBusinessServices.aspx
- https://sos.wyo.gov/business/docs/businessfees.pdf
