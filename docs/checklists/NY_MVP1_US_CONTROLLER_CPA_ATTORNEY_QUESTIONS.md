# NY MVP-1 US Controller — CPA / Attorney Question Set

Date: 2026-09-24

Status: PREPARATION_ONLY

Purpose: obtain fact-specific professional answers before any real P1 authorization.

This is a question set, not legal or tax advice.

## Entity / federal tax

Ask the CPA/attorney to confirm, based on the actual owner and entity facts:

1. What is the US federal tax classification of the formed entity by default?
2. Are any elections recommended or required for the intended activity?
3. What federal returns or information filings are required for a foreign-owned single-member LLC?
4. Does Form 5472 apply, and if so what transactions/events must be tracked?
5. Is a pro-forma Form 1120 required with Form 5472 for this fact pattern?
6. What are the filing deadlines and extension mechanics?
7. What records must be maintained to support related-party/reportable transactions?
8. Does the owner need an ITIN for the intended operations, or can the required compliance be completed without one?
9. What is the correct EIN process for the actual foreign owner?
10. What US-source / effectively connected income issues should be reviewed for the planned service-fee model?

## Wyoming

11. What recurring Wyoming state filings/fees apply after formation?
12. Does the intended activity create any Wyoming tax or licensing obligation beyond ordinary LLC maintenance?
13. Is the registered-agent/address arrangement appropriate for the intended operating model?

## New York nexus / foreign qualification

14. Could accessing NY OSC records, serving New York owners/representatives, contracting with New York customers, using New York counsel or other planned activities create New York nexus?
15. Under what actual facts would the Wyoming LLC need to qualify/register to do business in New York?
16. Are there New York taxes, fees, licenses or consumer-service rules triggered by the planned workflow?
17. Does the planned unclaimed-property recovery/service model require any New York-specific contractual disclosures, registrations or fee limitations before outreach/agreement stages?
18. Which answers can be resolved before P1 and which depend on later outreach/contract design?

## Privacy / cross-border operations

19. If the US controller is genuinely US-based and P1 remains US-only, what privacy framework applies to the planned pre-contact identity/targetability research?
20. What changes if an EU-based individual accesses live owner PII?
21. What changes if an EU contractor, employee, agent or stable arrangement participates in operations?
22. What operational separation is appropriate between US live-PII production activity and EU synthetic/non-PII development?
23. What retention/minimization controls should apply to transient owner PII during P1?
24. Is any privacy notice or other transparency step required before the specific pre-contact research planned for P1?
25. Are there restrictions on using public web search for identity/contactability research for this commercial purpose?

## Customer / fee model

26. Which entity should sign customer/LSP service agreements?
27. Which entity should receive fees?
28. Are contingency/success-based fees permitted for the intended service and jurisdiction, and what limitations/disclosures apply?
29. Are there attorney/non-attorney fee-sharing or referral constraints relevant to using independent counsel in the recovery workflow?
30. At what stage may the platform communicate directly with an owner, heir, beneficiary or representative without creating unauthorized-practice or other licensing issues?

## Deliverable requested from professional

Request a short written memo or email that separates:

- confirmed facts;
- assumptions;
- legal/tax conclusions;
- unresolved questions;
- actions required before real P1;
- actions that can wait until outreach/contract/claim stages.

The project must not convert an unresolved professional question into an AI assumption.
